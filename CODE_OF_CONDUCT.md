# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant].

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and
accept it gracefully.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to
the maintainer at **<jianlingzh@gmail.com>**. Reports are confidential.

Maintainers will review and act on reports within a reasonable timeframe.
Consequences may include warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal
from the project's spaces, at the maintainers' discretion.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
